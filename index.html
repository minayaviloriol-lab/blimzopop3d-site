BLIMZOPOP 3D

BlimzoPop 3D Publisher

A creator utility for preparing original short-form animations and sending video drafts to TikTok for review by the creator.

What it does

  • Connects to TikTok only after the account owner authorizes access.
  • Uses basic profile information to identify the connected account.
  • Can send a video draft to TikTok for the creator to review.
  • The creator decides whether and when to publish a video.
